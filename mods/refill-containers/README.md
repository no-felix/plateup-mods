# Refill Containers

Refill a matching empty tray or pot with the normal grab action. Hold a full or partially full container and use it on an empty one. The food moves to the counter and the empty container returns to your hand.

Combiners use the same rule. The returned empty container stays on the combiner until removed. Food variants, toppings and remaining portions are preserved. Combining two non-empty containers keeps the base game's behavior.

## Supported food

- Cookies, brownies, cupcakes and flavoured sponge cakes.
- Taco trays, including all topping combinations.
- Proofed doughnut trays and cooked lasagne.
- Cooked beans, bamboo, broccoli, mashed potato, bolognese, roux, mushroom sauce, red sauce and drained spaghetti in pots.
- Chocolate, strawberry and vanilla homemade ice cream mixtures in mixing bowls.

Soups that leave drained broth, food remnants, dirty dishes and single-portion recipes are excluded. The target must contain only the matching empty carrier. Transfers that violate holder filters or locks are refused.

There are no KitchenLib or Harmony dependencies, new items, or custom saved components. Host-only multiplayer, save/load compatibility and compatibility with other gameplay mods have not been verified.

## Installation

Copy `content/KitchenRefillContainers.dll` from the release package into a named folder under `PlateUp/Mods`, then restart PlateUp with mods enabled. Keep only one local or Workshop copy installed. To uninstall, close the game and remove that mod folder.

See the repository [build instructions](https://github.com/no-felix/plateup-mods#build-and-test) and [test coverage](https://github.com/no-felix/plateup-mods/blob/main/docs/TESTING.md) for development and verification.
